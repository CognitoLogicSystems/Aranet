What is a Jello Audit?

A Jello Audit maps how opportunity actually moves through your operation. We examine five rough estimates from your last 30 days to identify exactly where prospective customers stall or disappear.

Lead → Call → Estimate → Job → Invoice → Review → Referral

The 48-72 hour turnaround

  • One 45-minute discovery session: We collect the metrics that matter most for your pipeline.
  • Pipeline flow trace: A visual view of how the customer journey really behaves.
  • Leak and dollar calculation: We quantify the biggest drop-off points and potential lost revenue.
  • Three zero-software moves: Immediate operational actions you can run without buying tools.
